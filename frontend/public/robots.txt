# axelnovaventures.com — crawler policy. See docs/global/SEO-AND-DISCOVERY.md.
#
# The AI crawler groups below are a deliberate choice: the studio WANTS to be
# retrievable and citable by AI answer engines (ChatGPT search, Claude,
# Perplexity, Google AI Overviews), so each one gets the same Allow/Disallow
# set as every other crawler. Two of them also permit TRAINING use —
# Google-Extended (Gemini) and CCBot (Common Crawl, which feeds many training
# sets). To keep search/answer bots but opt out of training, delete just those
# two groups (or change their Allow to a Disallow: /). The private surfaces
# (admin, portals, proposals, the inquiry confirmation) are disallowed for
# everyone and are noindex besides.

User-agent: *
Allow: /
Disallow: /admin
Disallow: /portal
Disallow: /proposals
Disallow: /investor
Disallow: /quote/preview
Disallow: /quote/success
