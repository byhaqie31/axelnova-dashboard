# Axel Nova Ventures: public site copy

The current copy for the public site, written and adopted 29 September 2026 from a review of the previous copy, the Nuxt page files and the cached public pages. Where the copy makes a commercial or operational promise, confirm the underlying terms before publishing.

## Editorial direction

- Keep the brand line **“Crafted by design. Built to last.”** It is distinctive; the SEO title and first paragraph can explain the service and location.
- Use **I** for Qie's personal work, process and replies. Use **Axel Nova Ventures** for company identity. Use **we** only for a team or partner process that actually involves more than Qie.
- Replace most em dashes with full stops, commas or colons. A dash is fine when it genuinely improves rhythm; repeating one in every paragraph makes the site harder to read.
- Name the service, customer and outcome in ordinary words. Keep technical terms where they help a technical buyer, chiefly in service details and case studies.
- Keep page titles and descriptions distinct. The suggested lengths below are editing choices, not Google limits.

## SEO setup and implementation priorities

1. **Publish consistent facts first.** Check the phone number, reply promise, package names, referral eligibility, payment terms, support periods and all named integrations. The cached live page showed `+60 17-710 9486`; the checked-in contact page and business schema say `+60 18-317 3103`. Confirm the correct number, then use it consistently in visible copy, click links and structured data.
2. **Map each page to one main search need.** The homepage serves Kuala Lumpur web design and development; `/services` covers the offer and pricing; individual service pages explain one service; projects provide proof; About and Company establish who is behind the work. Do not repeat the same keyword phrase in every heading.
3. **Use unique, accurate titles and descriptions.** Google has no fixed 60/155 character rule and may rewrite either result element. Edit for clarity and likely display width. Replace automatic clipping of admin descriptions with deliberate summaries where possible.
4. **Keep the existing SEO foundation.** The repository already has server-rendered titles and descriptions, canonical URLs, an XML sitemap, robots rules, Open Graph tags and business/service/article structured data. Validate the deployed HTML and sitemap after publishing because the accessible cached public pages show older copy than the repository.
5. **Fix actual indexability issues.** The project detail page currently shows a “not found” state without setting a 404 status. Missing slugs should return HTTP 404. Check that the production sitemap lists only public, indexable, canonical 200-status URLs. Confirm the `www` hostname redirects permanently to the chosen apex domain rather than serving a duplicate site. A page blocked in `robots.txt` cannot expose its `noindex` tag to Google; use authentication for private content and review how public success or token pages are excluded.
6. **Use structured data for facts, not decoration.** Keep the `ProfessionalService`, `Service`, `BlogPosting` and breadcrumb data aligned with visible content. `FAQPage` markup is unlikely to produce FAQ rich results for a studio site; keep useful FAQs for visitors rather than for a promised search feature.
7. **Measure after launch.** Verify the domain in Google Search Console, submit the sitemap once, inspect the main pages for indexing and monitor queries, clicks and page experience over time. Improve pages based on real search terms and enquiries, not a target keyword density.
